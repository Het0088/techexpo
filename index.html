How to Use This Gallery

👀

Observe Carefully

Pay attention to hand shape, position, and movement for each sign.

  • Watch hand position relative to body
  • Note which fingers are extended
  • Check palm orientation
👏

Practice Regularly

Repeat each sign multiple times until smooth.

  • Practice 10-15 minutes daily
  • Start with alphabet, then numbers
  • Master one category before moving on
🎥

Record Yourself

Use the live demo to check recognition quality.

  • Enable webcam in Live Demo
  • Perform signs in good lighting
  • Check real-time accuracy
🧑‍🏫

Get Feedback

  • Join community forums
  • Attend virtual practice sessions
  • Connect with instructors

Learning Tips for Beginners

Start with Fingerspelling

ISL alphabet (A-Z) builds your foundation.

Practice drill: Spell 5 new words daily

Master Numbers Early

Numbers 0-9 are used everywhere.

Practice drill: Count to 100, tell time

Learn Common Phrases

“Hello”, “Thank you”, “Please”, “How are you?”

Practice drill: Use 3 new phrases daily

Practice with Others

Friends, family, or online community.

Practice drill: 10-minute sessions weekly

Focus on Hand Shape

Hand shape, position, and movement basics.

Practice drill: 5 hand shapes daily

Be Consistent

15 minutes daily beats 2 hours once a week.

Practice drill: Set a daily reminder

Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Signing Too Fast

Take time to form each sign correctly.

✅ Solution: Practice slowly until natural.
❌ Incorrect Hand Position

Keep hands in the “signing space” (waist to above head).

✅ Solution: Practice in front of chest.
❌ Ignoring Facial Expressions

Facial expressions carry grammar and emotion.

✅ Solution: Practice in front of a mirror.
❌ Not Using Live Demo

Without feedback, bad habits stick.

✅ Solution: Use Live Demo regularly.
